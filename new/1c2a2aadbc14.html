Sunshine Mountain Ford carries a broad new vehicle inventory at our Nanaimo location, so drivers across Vancouver Island can compare several models, configurations and colours in one place. Our inventory is updated online as vehicles arrive and sell.

Our truck inventory includes Ford F-150 pickups in a range of cab and box configurations, Ford Super Duty trucks for heavy towing and commercial use, mid-size Ford Rangers, compact Ford Mavericks, and the all-electric F-150 Lightning. Whether you need a work truck, a tow vehicle or a daily driver with a bed, there are several options to compare.

For drivers shopping for a new SUV in Nanaimo, our inventory includes the compact Ford Escape and Bronco Sport, the three-row Ford Explorer, the off-road Ford Bronco and the electric Mustang Mach-E. Most are available with all-wheel or four-wheel drive.

We carry Ford Transit cargo vans in various configurations, the electric E-Transit, and chassis cab units ready for upfitting. Our commercial vehicle centre handles fleet purchases, commercial financing and upfit planning.

Our new car selection includes the Ford Mustang, offered as a fastback or a convertible, with a V8 or a turbocharged four-cylinder engine.

Drivers shopping for an electric vehicle in Nanaimo can compare the F-150 Lightning, the Mustang Mach-E and the E-Transit at our dealership, along with hybrid and plug-in hybrid models including the F-150 hybrid, the Maverick hybrid and the plug-in Escape. Ask about current incentive programs when you are ready to buy, since those change from year to year.

Three decisions shape whether a truck suits you: cab size, box length and drivetrain. A crew cab with a shorter box works for most families and job sites, while a longer box earns its length if you regularly haul sheet goods or lumber. Get those three right and the rest is preference.

Vehicles already built and in transit to the dealership are listed as incoming, and they can be reserved before they arrive. If nothing currently on the lot is quite right, ask what is on its way.
